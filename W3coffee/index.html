Follow the Winding Road to The Coffee Oasis

We’re a little out of the way, but take a drive in the country down Garrett Road to The Coffee Oasis today! Indulge in our locally roasted free-trade coffee and home-made pastries. You’ll feel right at home at The Coffee Oasis!

Friendly and eclectic — The Coffee Oasis is the perfect place to take a break, enjoy a refreshing beverage, and have a snack or light meal.

The Coffee Oasis features:

  • Specialty Coffee and Organic Tea
  • Bagels, Muffins, and Gluten-free Pastries
  • Organic Salads
  • Music and Poetry Readings
  • Open Mic Night
